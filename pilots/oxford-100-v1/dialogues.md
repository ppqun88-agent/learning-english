## 核对银行账单 (pilot01-bank)

pilot01-bank-s02. **Adviser:** Let us check the amount and the name of the company.
pilot01-bank-s03. **Customer:** It might be my annual subscription, but I thought I had cancelled it.
pilot01-bank-s04. **Adviser:** Do you have a record of the cancellation?
pilot01-bank-s05. **Customer:** Yes, I have an email. Can I request a refund?
pilot01-bank-s06. **Adviser:** You can contact the company first. Keep a copy of its response in case you need to raise a dispute.
